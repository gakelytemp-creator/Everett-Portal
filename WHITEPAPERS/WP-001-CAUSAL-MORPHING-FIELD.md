# Everett Portal White Paper WP-001

## Morphing as a Causal Field

**Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Project:** Everett Portal  
**Class:** World Genesis / Divergence Model

> **Morphing is a field, not a slider.**

---

## Abstract

Everett Portal requires an alternate historical branch that separates from a shared past without becoming an arbitrary collection of invented facts.

A single global “0–100% alternate” slider is too crude. Historical divergence does not reach every person, institution, city, technology, and cultural field at the same speed.

This paper proposes **causal morphing**: a field defined over the world’s causal graph.

A node close to the Point of Divergence may separate rapidly from our history. A causally distant node may remain almost unchanged for years or decades.

The purpose of the field is not to interpolate facts. It is to determine **where and when the consequences of divergence have become active**.

---

## 1. Shared Trunk and Point of Divergence

Let the two histories share a common trunk up to a Point of Divergence:

**POD**

Before the POD, both branches use the same historical substrate.

After the POD, neither branch is treated as the privileged “original.” They are two continuations of a shared past.

The project may use our recorded history as a reference dataset, but the alternate branch is not defined as a cosmetic modification of that dataset. It is a separate causal continuation.

---

## 2. Why a Global Morph Percentage Fails

A global parameter such as:

`morph = 37%`

cannot express realistic historical divergence.

If one event changes the life of a musician, the first effects may appear in:

- family and collaborators;
- recording schedules;
- record labels;
- nearby musicians;
- journalists;
- audiences;
- later cultural movements.

At the same moment, unrelated agricultural policy or a distant local institution may remain almost identical to our branch.

Historical divergence is therefore **non-uniform**.

---

## 3. The Morph Field

Define:

`M(v, t) ∈ [0,1]`

where:

- `v` = a node in the causal graph;
- `t` = time;
- `M = 0` means the node still follows the shared/reference trajectory as closely as the branch rules allow;
- `M = 1` means the node is fully governed by the alternate branch’s causal history.

The important point is that `M` belongs to a **node and time**, not to the universe as one global scalar.

Examples of nodes:

- a person;
- a band;
- a newspaper;
- a city music scene;
- a political movement;
- an industry;
- a technology;
- an institution;
- an artwork;
- a social practice.

---

## 4. Divergence Propagates Through Causal Links

Morphing spreads through relations such as:

`event → person → work → audience → new creator → institution`

The field changes because causal influence reaches new nodes.

It does **not** change merely because the calendar advances.

Two nodes at the same date may therefore have radically different morph values.

This gives Everett Portal a divergence **front** rather than a universal clock-based fade.

---

## 5. Numerical and Semantic Layers

Two different mechanisms are required.

### Numerical variables

Some quantities may be interpolated or continuously transformed when the model justifies it.

Examples:

- market share;
- audience size;
- popularity;
- demographic proportions;
- production volume.

### Semantic facts

Discrete facts must not be numerically interpolated.

A person is not “30% alive.”

A concert did not “40% happen.”

A band was either formed or not formed.

For semantic facts, the morph field controls **which causal regime determines the fact**, not a percentage inside the fact itself.

---

## 6. Direction Is Parameter-Specific

Not every branch parameter moves from 0 to 1.

Some tendencies may strengthen:

`0 → 1`

Others may disappear:

`1 → 0`

The parameter vector may therefore contain many independently directed components.

A branch is better described as:

`Θ(t) = {m1(t), m2(t), ... mn(t)}`

than by one universal morph percentage.

---

## 7. Invariants

Causal morphing must preserve:

1. **Shared past integrity** — pre-POD history is not silently rewritten.
2. **Discrete fact integrity** — semantic events remain discrete.
3. **Causal locality** — divergence reaches a node through an explainable path.
4. **Reproducibility** — the same world version and coordinate must produce the same structural state.
5. **Provenance** — alternate-branch artifacts remain marked as alternate-branch artifacts.
6. **No privileged branch** — both post-POD histories are continuations of a shared trunk.

---

## 8. What the Field Is For

The morph field is not an aesthetic effect.

It answers a technical question:

> **Which parts of the world have already become causally dependent on the alternate history at time t?**

That answer constrains all later world generation.

A newspaper, interview, radio broadcast, or album can only refer to alternate facts that exist inside the causal field that has reached them.

---

## 9. Open Problems

This model does not eliminate semantic judgment.

The system must still estimate:

- causal strength;
- propagation delay;
- domain coupling;
- which historical dependencies are relevant;
- when a node has crossed from reference-dominated to branch-dominated behavior.

These judgments are fallible and must remain inspectable.

Morphing organizes divergence.

It does not magically solve historical causality.

---

## 10. Core Statement

> **Facts do not morph. Causal dependence does.**

That distinction is the foundation of the Everett Portal divergence model.
