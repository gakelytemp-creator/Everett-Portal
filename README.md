## What is Everett Portal?

Everett Portal is an experimental cultural archive built around a simple question:

**What could we learn if we could compare our history with another internally consistent history that diverged from ours?**

The project does not rewrite our past and does not present generated artifacts as lost originals.

Instead, it constructs alternative historical branches and allows cultural fragments from those branches to cross a fictional portal into our world.

Music, radio broadcasts, newspapers, magazines, interviews, paintings, posters, literature and other cultural artifacts may pass through the portal.

Their origin must always remain visible.

---

## The First Rule: Do Not Lie About Origin

Our timeline is never corrected retroactively.

An artifact created today cannot be declared an authentic artifact from our own past.

Every transmitted object has provenance:

`Origin → Branch → Time → Context → Portal Event`

The difference between reconstruction and falsification is not the shape of the artifact.

It is its **time and causal origin**.

A mark made today cannot become a mark made three thousand years ago simply because it looks identical.

Everett Portal therefore treats provenance as part of the artifact itself.

## The Portal Mark

Artifacts originating from an alternate branch may carry the small mark:

### Ǝ

`Ǝ` functions like a quiet portal stamp.

It should make transmitted material recognizable without making reading, listening or viewing difficult.

The mark may appear in text, metadata, archive labels, newspapers, visual works and other media.

A fragment removed from its original context should still carry some trace of where it came from.

Everett Portal does not attempt to simulate an entire Earth in advance.

The world is generated **causally and on demand**.

A divergence creates a new historical field.

Only the parts required to support a requested artifact are expanded.

The basic model is:

**Deterministic World → Probabilistic Creativity → Deterministic Identity → Permanent Memory**

The reproducible part of history should emerge from the same world parameters in the same way.

Creative acts may contain stochastic generation.

But once an artifact is accepted into branch history, it has one birth, one provenance and a persistent identity.

Changing its title, date, genre or presentation must never create the illusion of a second original work.

## The Puzzle

The alternate world may be imagined as a vast multidimensional puzzle.

Most cells remain unopened.

A cell may represent a particular:

- time;
- place;
- cultural field;
- person or institution;
- event;
- scale of observation.

Opening one cell may require calculation of several causal predecessors.

Once revealed, those dependencies become part of the persistent branch history.

We do not need to store every possible newspaper, conversation or event.

We need to preserve enough structure for the same world to be reconstructed consistently.

**The portal is expensive. Only a small part of the other world crosses at a time.**

---

## Portal Requests

People in our world may fund the opening of unexplored regions of the puzzle.

They may ask:

> What was happening in the Manchester music scene in 1988?

or:

> What did a cultural newspaper in Tokyo look like in 1994?

Funding selects **the question, coordinate and computational work**.

It does not purchase the answer.

**You may fund a coordinate.  
You may not buy an outcome.**

Artifacts may also arrive without being requested, as transmissions initiated from inside the alternate branch.

Eventually, exchange may become reciprocal: the other branch may request cultural artifacts that exist in our history but never appeared in theirs.

## Everett Radio

Everett Radio is one of the primary windows through the portal.

It is not a radio station explaining an alternate world to us.

It is radio **from that world**.

Its presenters talk to their own listeners.

They mention their own musicians, concerts, arguments, advertisements, cultural memories and ordinary events without stopping to explain them to us.

The listener discovers the world through differences.

A broadcast may lead to an interview.

The interview may reveal an earlier concert.

The concert may reveal a forgotten band.

The band may lead back to a divergence decades earlier.

The archive grows by following these causal paths.

When Noepedia is used, a generator should receive only the task-relevant branch cut it needs. It should not own or silently rewrite the persistent world state.

The portal is not limited to radio or music.

Possible transmitted artifacts include:

- 🎵 music and albums;
- 📻 radio broadcasts;
- 📰 newspapers;
- 📖 magazines;
- 🎨 paintings and drawings;
- 🖼️ exhibition catalogues;
- 📷 photographs;
- 📚 books and manuscripts;
- 🎭 posters and advertisements;
- 🎙️ interviews;
- 🎟️ concert archives;
- ✉️ cultural documents.

A newspaper may reveal more of a world than a formal historical summary: music beside politics, fashion beside prices, advertisements beside arguments, ordinary life beside major events.

The archive therefore grows through **fragments**, not by manufacturing a complete encyclopedia in advance.

## Why Differences Matter

Everett Portal is not designed to tell people what to think.

Its purpose is to provide another viewpoint from which familiar things can become visible again.

One world says:

**“This is normal.”**

Another world says:

**“Imagine such a strange thing.”**

Placed beside one another, they create a difference that neither world can provide alone.

The portal works less like a teacher delivering an answer and more like an instrument for making differences observable.

Sometimes one world is not enough to understand the meaning of an individual, an event or a cultural path.

The second world supplies the missing comparison.

## Founders

The project has founders on both sides of the portal.

Founders in our world build the real infrastructure, protocols and archive.

Founders represented in the alternate branch become part of its internal history and may initiate transmissions from their side.

Founders build and maintain the portal.

They do not own the alternate world.

They may establish protocols, preserve provenance, maintain continuity and account for resources.

They may not arbitrarily rewrite historical outcomes.

> **We founded the portal. We did not found the world.**

## Noepedia and the Consistency Gate

Everett Portal's Consistency Gate should not be confused with Noepedia's Socratic Daimonion.

The **Daimonion** is the general transaction boundary around the persistent Noepedia field.

The **Consistency Gate** is an Everett-specific admission service used when an event or artifact seeks entry into one branch's canon.

The Daimonion may invoke exact typed checks, causal-neighborhood retrieval, identity/one-birth checks, semantic comparison, novelty checks, confidence estimation, or escalation. The gate remains fallible and measurable.

A crucial boundary is:

> **CANON_IN_BRANCH ≠ OBSERVED_IN_OUR_WORLD**

A canonical Everett fact is binding inside that branch/version. It is not thereby evidence about our actual history.

See [NOEPEDIA_INTEGRATION.md](NOEPEDIA_INTEGRATION.md) and the detailed [Noepedia Everett pilot](https://github.com/gakelytemp-creator/Noepedia/blob/main/PILOT_EVERETT_PORTAL.md).

## Funding and Accounting

The project is intended to maintain transparent accounting.

A person may contribute money, computation, hosting, editing, generation costs or other resources toward a particular transmission or piece of infrastructure.

Such contributions should be traceable.

Where a funded artifact or program later produces revenue, recoverable contributions may be repaid according to transparent rules.

Funding must not create ownership over history or the right to dictate outcomes.

**Contribution buys neither truth nor control of the branch.**
